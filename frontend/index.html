DATA INTELLIGENCE

Smart Expense Insights

Empirical patterns and key cost drivers discovered from 1,000 surveyed households.

🏠
Dominant Driver · r = 0.945

Size & Bedroom Impact

House size and bedroom count have the strongest correlation with monthly expenses (r = 0.945 & 0.941). Each additional bedroom adds an average of ₹2,596 per month.

⚡
Utility Threshold · 329 Units

Electricity Usage Premium

Homes consuming above the dataset average (329 units) incur an average monthly expense of ₹17,297 vs ₹10,340 for lower consumption homes — a ₹6,957 difference.

👨‍👩‍👧
Occupancy Curve · +₹2,095/Person

Family Size Scaling

Expenses scale steadily from ₹7,215 for single-person homes up to ₹21,878 for 8 occupants, averaging an additional ₹2,095 in baseline operational costs per person.

📊
Middle 50% · ₹10.5k – ₹17.5k

Benchmark Expense Tiers

The interquartile range shows 50% of surveyed homes spend between ₹10,481 (25th percentile) and ₹17,512 (75th percentile), with an overall median of ₹13,962.

🤖
R² = 96.09% · MAE = ₹664.55

Model Explanatory Power

Linear Regression achieves a genuine 96.09% R² and ₹824.76 RMSE on unseen test data, proving that size, occupancy, and utilities reliably explain expense variances.
